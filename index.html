Meet people: activities around me

Au pairs make friends during training week and in their LCC cluster, but new au pairs land every week and there is no easy way to meet them. After arrival, most of us barely open the app.

This new Meet tab turns the app into the place to find things to do with other au pairs nearby. It is built around activities, not profiles, so it stays friendly and safe rather than feeling like a dating app.

Try it

  • Tap Meet in the bottom bar.
  • Open an activity and tap Join. A group chat appears in Chat.
  • Tap + Create to post your own activity.
  • Tap the area name to plan a trip, for example a weekend in Chicago.

Why it helps Cultural Care

  • Daily reasons to open the app after arrival.
  • Faster integration for new arrivals, fewer lonely first weeks.
  • LCCs can post cluster meetings and see who is engaged.

Concept by an au pair. Not an official Cultural Care product. All people and events are fictional.
